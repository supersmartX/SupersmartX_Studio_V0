## Minimum Sizes

- Digital: 24px height minimum
- Print: 10mm height minimum

## Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the cap height of the "S" in Studio. No other elements should enter this space.

## Don'ts

- Don't stretch or distort the logo
- Don't rotate the logo
- Don't change the logo colors
- Don't add effects (drop shadows, gradients, outlines)
- Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Don't crop the logo
