# SuperSmartX Studio Brand Guidelines v3

## Architecture
SuperSmartX is the parent brand.
SuperSmartX Studio is a product.
The Studio mark is a product identifier.

## Product-family rule
Every SuperSmartX product should have:
- a unique geometric mark
- a unique solid signature color
- shared minimal/geometric design language
- neutral parent-brand treatment

Example:
Tagly — #2457F5
Studio — #7C3AED

## Studio identity
Canonical product color: #7C3AED.

The approved Studio mark is the ribbon/film-strip S with the internal play shape from the approved Studio concept artwork.

The canonical logo is flat/solid in product purple. Marketing may use glow/gradient effects around the mark, but those effects are not part of the canonical logo.

## Clear space
Minimum clear space: 25% of mark width.

## Minimum size
24px preferred; 16px absolute minimum for favicon/UI.

## Asset policy
Use the exact approved asset files. Do not redraw or substitute a generic camera/play icon.
