# SuperSmartX Studio Brand Guidelines — Production v4

## Brand architecture
SuperSmartX is the parent brand. SuperSmartX Studio is a product.

## Signature
- Product accent: #7C3AED
- Primary dark canvas: #09090B
- Typeface: Inter
- UI weights: 400, 500, 600, 700

## Logo
The approved Studio mark is the ribbon/film-strip S with internal play shape. The exact approved artwork is preserved in this kit.

## Clear space
Minimum clear space = 25% of mark width on every side.

## Digital sizes
- 16px: favicon/tiny UI only
- 24px+: preferred UI usage
- 180px: Apple touch icon
- 192px: PWA icon
- 512px: PWA/high-density icon
- 1024px: general high-resolution digital export
- 2048px/4096px: high-resolution raster/print workflows

## Social
Open Graph master: 1200×630 px.

## Canonical vs marketing
The canonical mark must remain unchanged. Marketing layouts may use lighting/glow/gradient effects around the mark, but those effects are not part of the logo asset itself.

## Accessibility
Use sufficient contrast between logo and background. Do not rely on purple alone to communicate a UI state.

## File naming
Use lowercase, descriptive, hyphen-separated names and keep production web assets under public/brand/studio/.
